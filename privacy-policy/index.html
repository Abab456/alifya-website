Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Alif Ya (Android package: com.alifya.app). It explains how the app handles privacy and safety for children and beginners learning Arabic.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Overview

Alif Ya is designed to provide a simple and safe educational experience.

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • There is no advertising in the current version.
  • There is no analytics or behavioural tracking in the current version.
  • No personal information is collected, sold, or shared.

Device data and permissions

  • Learning progress and rewards remain on the device.
  • Audio is used only for educational playback.
  • No access is requested for location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Contact

If you have any privacy questions about Alif Ya, contact: info@mawqi3net.com
