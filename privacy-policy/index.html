Privacy Policy for Alif Ya

Android package: com.alifya.app

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Overview

Alif Ya is an educational Arabic-learning app for children and beginners. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

No user account is required to use Alif Ya.

Information We Collect

Alif Ya does not collect personal information from users.

  • No name, email address, phone number, or similar personal data is collected through the app.
  • No personal information is collected for profiling or marketing.

Local App Data

Progress and rewards, if saved, remain locally on the user’s device to support the learning experience.

This local data is not transmitted to us.

Children’s Privacy

Alif Ya is designed as an educational app for children and beginners. Because the app does not collect personal information, it is built to minimize data exposure for young users.

Parents or guardians may contact us with any privacy questions.

Permissions and Device Features

Audio is used only for educational playback.

Alif Ya does not request access to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone

Advertising and Analytics

The app does not currently display ads.

The app does not currently use analytics or behavioral tracking.

Third-Party Services

Alif Ya does not include third-party advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs for user tracking.

Note: The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which may process information according to Google’s own policies.

Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information from the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect personal information through Alif Ya, we do not retain personal information on our servers from app use.

Any app progress stored locally may be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app data on the device.

Security

We design Alif Ya to limit data collection and reduce privacy risk. Local device security controls (such as screen lock and device protections) also help protect any locally stored app progress.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@mawqi3net.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to Alif Ya (Android package: com.alifya.app).
